- Final targets used by exported models: `need_irrigation`, `is_favorable`, `suggested_hour`.

Adaptation script behavior checked in `adapt_irrigation_dataset.py`:

- Normalizes soil moisture values from fraction to percentage when needed.
- Converts wind direction labels such as `N`, `NE`, and `SSW` into degrees.
- Parses light-intensity suffixes such as `k` and `m`.
- Adds cyclic encodings for hour, month, and wind direction.
- Computes rule-based irrigation need and favorable-irrigation context.

Verified metadata values:

- `need_irrigation`: Random Forest, accuracy `0.9998099220680479`, macro-F1 `0.9996319305656356`, CV accuracy mean `0.9997909052420184`.
