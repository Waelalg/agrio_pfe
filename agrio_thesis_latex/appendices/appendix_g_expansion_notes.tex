\chapter{Thesis Expansion Notes for Final Editing}

When final figures are available, each placeholder should be replaced by a high-resolution image. Captions should remain under the figure, and the figure must be cited in the surrounding paragraph before it appears. For figures adapted from research papers, redraw the figure in the thesis style and cite the source in the caption. For screenshots and prototype photos produced by the students, use Source: Authors.

The final defense version may simplify the thesis architecture diagrams. The full detailed architecture is useful for the manuscript and appendix, but defense slides should use the split diagrams: global overview, physical communication, MQTT topics, backend internal architecture, Digital Twin processing, and deployment architecture.

The final experimental chapter should be updated with actual observations once the missing screenshots are captured. Each test should include input, expected result, observed result, evidence figure, and status. If a test was not performed, it must be marked as future validation rather than presented as completed.
