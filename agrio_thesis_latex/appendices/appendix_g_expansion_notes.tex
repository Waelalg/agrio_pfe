\chapter{Thesis Expansion Notes}

All manuscript figures should use stable source files, concise captions, and explicit citations when adapted from research papers. Screenshots and prototype photos produced by the students should be cited through the surrounding implementation context rather than treated as external sources.

Defense slides may simplify the thesis architecture diagrams. The manuscript uses split diagrams for the global overview, physical communication, MQTT topics, backend internal architecture, Digital Twin processing, and deployment architecture.

Experimental reporting should distinguish completed tests from future validation. Each completed test should include input, expected result, observed result, evidence figure, and status.
