Review, overview, systematic review, bibliometric review, and survey papers are not used as figure sources in the main technical chapters. Research-paper figures inserted in Part I are documented separately in \texttt{figures/research\_papers/}\linebreak[1]\texttt{FIGURE\_SOURCES.md}.
