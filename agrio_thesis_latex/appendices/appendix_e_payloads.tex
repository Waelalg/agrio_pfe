\begin{lstlisting}
{"device_id":"wemos1","D5":"OFF","D3":"OFF"}
\end{lstlisting}

Command examples: ON\_D5, OFF\_D5, ON\_D3, OFF\_D3, ALL\_ON, ALL\_OFF.
