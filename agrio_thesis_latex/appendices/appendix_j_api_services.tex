Mapping service & Resolves device identifiers, zones, and sensor types. \\
Realtime pipeline & Coordinates sensor health, state update, ML prediction, history, and recommendations. \\

Simulator & Computes what-if moisture projections. \\
ML service client & Sends feature payloads to the Random Forest inference service. \\
MAS service & Runs the multi-agent decision workflow and returns final decision, confidence, risk level, evidence, explanation, and warnings. \\
Recommendation engine & Transforms prediction/twin state into recommendations. \\
Forecast service & Calls weather forecast APIs and generates irrigation outlooks. \\
Device control service & Publishes MQTT commands to WeMos. \\
Irrigation scheduler & Executes scheduled irrigation events at the planned time. \\

\section{API evidence}

\begin{itemize}

\item Backend health or status response showing that the API service is reachable.

\item GET /api/v1/dashboard/overview or equivalent response showing dashboard aggregation.

\item GET /api/v1/zones/\{id\}/twin-state or equivalent showing virtual zone state.

\item POST simulation endpoint response showing projected moisture and recommendation.

\item POST /mas/decision response showing decision, explanation, confidence, risk level, evidence, and warnings.

\item POST device command endpoint response after ON\_D5/OFF\_D5 command.

\end{itemize}
